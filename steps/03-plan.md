# Step 3 — Plan the videos

Goal: a short, ordered list of videos the person can approve in one read. Write it in `PLAN.md` (from `templates/PLAN.md`).

1. **Order**: a hero video first (the product doing its main job end to end, 20 to 40 s), then one video per main
   feature (12 to 25 s each), ending with anything that ties surfaces together (desktop + phone).
2. **Each video is a tiny story** of 3 to 5 beats: the situation, the action, the result the viewer cares about.
   Write what is ON SCREEN for each beat, not marketing claims.
3. **Captions**: 2 to 4 per video, at most 5 words, plain words ("Invite your team", "It saves itself").
   No feature lists on screen; the picture explains.
4. **Surface per video**: web, desktop, phone, or two screens side by side (`steps/06-edit.md` shows layouts).
5. **Length and pace**: say roughly how long; waits will be sped up, not cut.
6. **Say what is not possible** with the current demo data or product (a feature that needs a real payment, a
   missing screen) instead of faking it.

Leave this step with: `PLAN.md` filled in, status DRAFT. Do not record yet: go to step 4.
