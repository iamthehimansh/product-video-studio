# Step 4 — ⛔ Ask for approval (two stops)

Recording takes time and the person knows their product and audience. Stop twice and wait for a clear yes.

## Stop 1: the plan (before any recording)
Send the person a short summary of `PLAN.md`:
- the demo story in one sentence,
- each video: number, name, surface, the beats in one line, the captions,
- anything you could not do and why,
- one question if something is genuinely open (order, which features).
Then end your turn and wait. Do not start recording "while they read".
When they answer, apply their changes to `PLAN.md`, write `Plan approved by <name> on <date>: "<their words>"`.

## Stop 2: the first test (before the real recordings)
Run the first video's record script in `probe` mode (and, if useful, one quick real take of the first beat) and show:
- 3 to 6 probe screenshots (the screens each beat will show),
- anything that looks wrong you are about to fix.
Wait for a yes again. Then record everything without stopping for each video.

If they change their mind later, update `PLAN.md` and re-record only what changed.
